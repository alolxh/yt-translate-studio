# YT Translate Studio

**Translate your YouTube videos' titles, descriptions and subtitles into many
languages — right inside YouTube Studio.**

YT Translate Studio is a Chrome extension built for YouTube creators who
publish for audiences in more than one language. Instead of copying text in
and out of separate tools, you translate and publish multilingual titles,
descriptions and caption tracks for your own videos without leaving YouTube
Studio.

## Who it is for

- Creators who re-publish or localize their videos for international viewers.
- Small channels and channel networks that need subtitles and metadata in
  several languages, quickly and consistently.

## What it does

- **Sign in with your own Google account** and pick the channel you work on.
- **Translate titles & descriptions** into your target languages and write
  them back to your videos through the official YouTube Data API v3.
- **Translate subtitles**: fetch the original caption track, translate it with
  the engine you choose, preview it, and publish the translated caption track.
- **Your choice of translation engine**: built-in free engines, your own API
  keys (DeepL, Google Cloud Translation), or a self-hosted/local server.
- **Language groups & content rules**: save reusable target-language presets
  and per-language wording rules for consistent output.
- **A dedicated translation workspace**: queue subtitle jobs for many videos,
  watch progress, and resume where you left off.

## Privacy

The extension only ever works on **your own channels**, with your explicit
Google sign-in. Your access token stays in your browser; we do not sell or
share your data. Read the full
[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html).
